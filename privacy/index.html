Privacy Policy

Gratuvia is designed around a simple principle: information about your work and earnings should stay under your control whenever possible.

Effective: September 5, 2026Last updated: September 5, 2026
Plain-language summary: Gratuvia's core mobile features are local-first. Core earnings records and original tip-slip images can be stored and processed on your device without cloud OCR. We do not sell your personal information or use behavioral advertising SDKs in the current app.

1. Who we are

Gratuvia is a product of Prokvik LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company ("Prokvik," "Gratuvia," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the Gratuvia mobile application and on gratuvia.app.

Privacy questions can be sent to admin@prokvik.com.

2. Information Gratuvia can handle on your device

Depending on the features you use, Gratuvia may store the following information locally on your device:

  • employers, positions, hourly wage rates, and work notes;
  • scheduled shifts, worked shifts, break times, and work history;
  • tips, tokes, gratuities, tip-share amounts, and related earnings records;
  • payroll settings, tax-estimation inputs, deductions, retirement contribution settings, pay periods, and paycheck estimates;
  • paycheck-history snapshots and, when supported, information you enter from actual paychecks or pay stubs;
  • original tip-slip images, image metadata, OCR text, OCR confidence information, parser results, and corrections you confirm;
  • preferences, notification settings, and app configuration; and
  • encrypted portable backup data when you choose to create a backup.

In the current local-first architecture, the phone is the authoritative source for these core records. Core use does not require cloud OCR or a network connection.

3. On-device OCR and tip-slip images

Gratuvia's private OCR feature is designed to process supported tip-slip images on the device. The app may create temporary working copies or crops for recognition, but the original selected or captured image is preserved separately as the user's record.

OCR results are suggestions. Gratuvia requires user review and confirmation before an OCR result should be treated as an authoritative earnings record. Core OCR processing is not designed to silently fall back to cloud OCR if the private on-device OCR engine is unavailable.

4. Local storage and security

Gratuvia's current local-first design stores core structured records in an encrypted database on the device. The database encryption key is generated on the device and stored using platform secure-storage facilities. Original tip-slip files are separately retained in the app's private document storage and are cryptographically hashed so Gratuvia can verify file integrity.

No security system can guarantee absolute protection. Device security also depends on factors outside our control, including your operating-system security, device passcode, backups, malware exposure, and physical access to your phone.

5. Encrypted portable backups

When you choose to export a Gratuvia portable backup, the app is designed to encrypt the backup on your device before you save or transfer it. The recovery key is presented separately and is not written into the encrypted backup file.

You choose where to store or share an exported backup. If you place it in a service such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, a NAS, or another storage provider, that provider's privacy and security practices apply to its handling of the encrypted file. Merely storing an exported backup with a provider you choose does not give Prokvik access to that backup.

Keep your recovery key safe. Prokvik may be unable to recover an encrypted backup if you lose the recovery key.

6. Information that may leave your device

Support and communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, attachments, and diagnostic details you voluntarily provide. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot, protect the service, and improve Gratuvia.

Website requests

When you visit gratuvia.app, our hosting and security providers may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website, such as IP address, browser or device type, requested pages, timestamps, and security-related request data. The current website does not use behavioral advertising.

Optional future online services

The Gratuvia codebase is designed so optional account, sync, or backup services can be added without making cloud storage a prerequisite for core mobile use. If we enable a feature that changes what personal data is transmitted to Prokvik or a service provider, we will update this Policy and provide any disclosures or choices required before that processing begins.

7. Device permissions

Gratuvia may request device permissions only when needed for a feature you choose to use. These can include:

  • Camera or photo access to capture or select tip slips and pay-stub images;
  • Photo-library or file access when you explicitly save, import, export, or share a record;
  • Notifications for schedule, missing-tip, or pay-period reminders when enabled; and
  • Sharing facilities when you choose the system share action.

Operating systems may describe these permissions differently. You can manage permissions in your device settings, although disabling a permission can make the related feature unavailable.

8. How we use information we receive

When Prokvik receives information through support, website operations, or a future optional online feature, we may use it to:

  • provide, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot Gratuvia;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms; and
  • improve Gratuvia using information you intentionally provide for that purpose.

9. Sale, advertising, and sharing

We do not sell personal information. The current Gratuvia mobile app does not include third-party behavioral advertising SDKs.

We may disclose information we actually possess to service providers that process it on our behalf, when required by law or valid legal process, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition subject to applicable law. Service providers are expected to protect information consistently with their role and applicable requirements.

10. Retention and deletion

Core local records remain on your device until you delete them, reset relevant app data, or remove the app, subject to how your device or operating-system backup system works. Exported backups remain wherever you chose to store them until you delete them there.

Support communications and limited website/security records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide support, maintain security, comply with law, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

The current local-first core does not require account creation. If a future release enables account creation or stores user records in a Prokvik-managed cloud service, Gratuvia will provide an appropriate account/data-deletion process and update this Policy before launch of that functionality.

11. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to enter information, grant optional permissions, create backups, send support materials, or use future optional online features. You can delete local records using available app controls and can remove exported backups from the storage location you selected.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide additional rights concerning personal information Prokvik actually holds about you. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Because local-only information is not held by Prokvik, we cannot access, correct, or delete information that exists only on your device or in a backup location controlled by you.

12. Children

Gratuvia is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the service. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the situation.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Gratuvia evolves, including when new online, subscription, backup, analytics, or account features are introduced. We will post the updated policy at this URL and update the effective date. When required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
