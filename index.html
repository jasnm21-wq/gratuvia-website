Tip and pay tracking for tipped workers

Track what you made.
Keep the record on your phone.

Gratuvia keeps tips, shifts, hourly wages, schedules, paycheck estimates, and tip slips in one place. The current Android build stores core records locally and runs OCR on the phone, so scanning a tip slip does not require a cloud OCR service.

What Gratuvia keeps together

Your shift, your tips, and the paycheck they turn into.

A tipped worker can end up with a schedule in one app, tip slips in the camera roll, wages in a note, and paycheck math in a calculator. Gratuvia keeps those records tied to the same work history.

$

Tips & shifts

Record gratuities, shift times, tip sharing, positions, employers, and hourly wages. Gratuvia uses those records to calculate earnings over time.

⌁

Tip-slip scanning

Scan a supported tip slip with OCR that runs on the phone. Review the amount before saving it, and keep the original image with the record.

▦

Schedule & calendar

Create recurring schedules, use different hours on different days, and edit or delete one scheduled shift without rewriting the whole week.

≈

Paycheck estimates

Estimate take-home pay from hourly wages, recorded tips, taxes, deductions, and retirement contributions you enter.

↺

Paycheck history

Keep a snapshot for each pay period instead of replacing the old estimate. Actual pay-stub amounts can later be compared with the estimate.

↗

Earnings history

Review daily, weekly, monthly, day-of-week, hourly, YTD, and annual totals from the shifts and gratuities you recorded.

Local-first by design

Your earnings history can stay on your device.

Gratuvia's core record system does not need cloud processing. Shifts, gratuities, schedules, payroll settings, OCR reviews, and original tip-slip images remain usable from the phone.
