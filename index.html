Work records in one place

One shift can affect your tips, hourly pay, and paycheck estimate.

Gratuvia ties those records together. You do not have to keep the schedule in one app, tip slips in the camera roll, wage notes somewhere else, and paycheck math in a calculator.

$

Tips & shifts

Record gratuities, shift times, tip sharing, positions, employers, and hourly wages. Gratuvia calculates earnings from the records you save.

⌁

Tip-slip scanning

Take a picture of a supported tip slip or upload one from your phone. Gratuvia reads the amount and other details, then lets you review them before saving. The original image stays attached to the record.

▦

Schedule & calendar

Create recurring schedules, set different hours for different days, and change one scheduled shift without rebuilding the whole week.

≈

Paycheck estimates

Estimate take-home pay from hourly wages, recorded tips, tax settings, deductions, and retirement contributions you enter.

↺

Paycheck history

Save a snapshot for each pay period instead of replacing the previous estimate. Actual pay-stub amounts can be compared with the estimate later.

↗

Earnings history

Review daily, weekly, monthly, day-of-week, hourly, YTD, and annual totals from the shifts and gratuities you recorded.

Local-first storage

Your work history does not need to live on our server.

The Android build keeps shifts, gratuities, schedules, payroll settings, scanned tip-slip details, and original tip-slip images usable from the phone. A network connection is not required for that core record system.
