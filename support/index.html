Frequently asked questions

Does Gratuvia upload my tip slips for OCR?

The current Android build runs OCR on the device. The original tip-slip record stays local unless you choose to export or share it.

What if OCR reads the wrong amount?

Do not confirm it. Correct the suggested field before saving. If the same document type fails more than once, send us the document type and a description of what Gratuvia read incorrectly.

Are paycheck and tax estimates exact?

No. Gratuvia calculates estimates from the wages, tips, tax settings, deductions, and retirement contributions you enter. Compare important amounts with the pay stub your employer issues.

Where are my records stored?

Core records in the current mobile architecture are stored locally on the phone in encrypted app storage. The Privacy & Security page explains the database, OCR, original images, and backup format.

How do backups work?

Gratuvia can create a portable encrypted backup that you save to a location you choose. The recovery key is separate from the backup file. Keep both.

How do I delete my data?

Delete local records with the controls available in the app. Exported backup files must be deleted from the storage location where you saved them. The current core app does not require an account.

Is Gratuvia available on iPhone?

Not yet. The Android beta is being prepared first. iOS is planned after the Android release is stable.
